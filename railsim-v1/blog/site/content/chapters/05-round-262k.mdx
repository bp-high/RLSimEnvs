## Round 2: give it room to think

If the problem was room, the fix was room. Tinker also serves Inkling 2 Small with a **262,144-token** context (256K, as Tinker lists it: 256 × 1,024). We
ran it at maximum effort with **32,768** output tokens per turn.

### Before any training: 0.259

More than double the 64K baseline, and better than the model we had just trained. 25 of 45 plans were legal (9
before) and two matched the proven optimum. The lesson of round 1 held: the bottleneck was the budget, not the model.

<Sidenote>
  For comparison, the larger models on the board had a 1M-token context and 64,000 output tokens per turn: about four
  times this room again.
</Sidenote>

### After 12 steps of RL: 0.316

Episodes here are about 143,000 tokens long, so each step played 8 tasks × 4 instead of 16 × 4. After 12 steps the
fine-tuned model scores **0.316**: **+0.056 per task**, better on 22 tasks, worse on 13, the same on 10. This time it
was not about budget (the model already submitted almost every time) but about plan quality. Legal plans now cost
**23 %** more than the optimum on the median, against **37 %** before, and three reached the optimum.
