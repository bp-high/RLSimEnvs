## The model and the training loop

**Inkling 2 Small** [@inkling2026] is a mixture-of-experts model from Thinking Machines Lab: 594 billion parameters,
17 billion active per token. Tinker trains a LoRA adapter on it [@hu2021lora], a small set of extra weights, while
Thinking Machines runs the hardware. We used rank 8 throughout.

The loop is plain policy-gradient reinforcement learning with group-relative advantages, in the spirit of GRPO
[@shao2024deepseekmath]:

1. pick a handful of training tasks;
2. let the model play each one **4 times** with the current weights;
3. compare the four scores: attempts that beat their siblings are pushed up, worse ones pushed down;
4. one optimizer step, then repeat with the new weights.

Every token the model wrote in an episode (its reasoning, its tool calls, its final plan) is one training sequence,
and the reward is the environment's own score. There are no human labels and no judge model.

<Sidenote>
  An episode is one sequence because each turn's prompt is the previous prompt, the tokens the model sampled and the
  new tool results, token for token. A turn cut off at the token limit is closed with the model's own end-of-turn
  tokens instead of being re-written.
</Sidenote>

<Accordion title="The details: rules of an episode and of a training step">

- **An episode:** 12 turns at most, `check_plan` up to 10 times, `submit_plan` once. Two turns in a row without a tool
  call end it; no plan scores 0. After every turn the model is told how many turns and how much context it has left,
  and when another check would no longer fit beside the final answer the next turn is announced as the last.
- **A step:** 16 tasks × 4 episodes (64K run) or 8 tasks × 4 (262K run); rewards centred within each task's group;
  groups whose four rewards are equal carry no signal and are skipped; one `importance_sampling` step at learning
  rate 1e-4.
- **Tracking progress honestly:** 16 training tasks are held out and played once every 6 steps; the 45 evaluation
  tasks are only played before and after.
- **A spending cap** stops the run before a step that would pass it.

</Accordion>
