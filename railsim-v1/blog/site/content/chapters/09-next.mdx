## What's next

1. **Settle round two:** one more rollout of both 262K evaluations, before and after.
2. **Train longer at 262K:** round one needed about a dozen steps before anything moved; round two stopped at twelve.
3. **Try high effort at 262K:** perhaps the room matters and maximum effort does not.
4. **Teach the length of a closure:** the 31 May regression suggests the model has not learned when waiting beats
   turning back.

## Try it

Everything is open:

- **The environment** and its tasks, on the [RailSimEnv Space](https://huggingface.co/spaces/bpHigh/RailSimEnv): an
  OpenEnv server you can connect an agent to, an eval explorer, a train graph, and a 3D replay whose first tab plays
  every Inkling 2 Small plan from this post.
- **The code** on [GitHub](https://github.com/bp-high/RLSimEnvs): the environment, the Tinker harness and the training
  loop (`railsim-v1/train/train_rl.py`), and every evaluation run with each plan the model submitted.
