## What it took

All of the training ran on Tinker; the loop, the environment and the grader ran on one small cloud machine. In tokens
and time:

| | 64K run (`rl-a`) | 256K run (`rl-b`) |
|---|---:|---:|
| RL steps | 23 | 12 |
| episodes per step | 16 tasks × 4 | 8 tasks × 4 |
| training episodes | 1,472 | 384 |
| tokens sampled (with validation) | 28.4M | 46.5M |
| tokens trained on | 62.9M | 57.5M |
| average training sequence | 44k tokens | 143k tokens |
| hours of sampling / of training | 3.0 / 0.5 | 6.0 / 0.5 |

Each evaluation of 45 tasks took 7 minutes at 64K and 30 minutes at 256K, with all 45 episodes in flight at once.
At 256K most of the training cost is training itself: the sequences are three times longer and long-context training
is priced higher per token.
